# Master Research References & Bibliography

This document maintains the master registry of verified scientific literature, institutional handbooks, national and international standards, and patent databases cited across the project repository.

## Citations Policy
- **Priority Hierarchy**: Central Silk Board (CSB) / ICAR > Peer-reviewed journals > International standards (ISO, BIS) > Official patent offices (WIPO, EPO, CGPDTM).
- **Zero Fabrication**: No URL, author name, or paper title may be invented. If a digital link or specific edition is pending verification, it is flagged explicitly as `Source required`.
