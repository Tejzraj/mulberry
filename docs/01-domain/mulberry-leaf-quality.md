# Mulberry Leaf Quality

## 1. What It Is
Mulberry leaf quality refers to the biochemical, physiological, and physical condition of the leaf blade, encompassing moisture content, crude protein, soluble carbohydrates, fiber content, turgor pressure, and absence of physical degradation.

## 2. Why It Matters
Silkworms obtain more than 70% of their metabolic water directly from ingested leaves. Bruised, shredded, or wilted leaves lose moisture rapidly; silkworms feed sluggishly on wilted leaves, resulting in stunted larval development, prolonged instar durations, and poor silk yields.

## 3. Key Concepts
- **Moisture Content**: Optimal harvested leaf moisture is 70%–80% for young instars (Chawki) and 65%–70% for late instars.
- **Biochemical Composition**: High-quality leaves typically contain 22%–27% crude protein, 10%–14% soluble sugars/carbohydrates, 2%–3% minerals/ash, and less than 10% crude fiber on a dry matter basis (CSRTI benchmarks).
- **Physical Cleanliness**: Absence of dust, soil particles, chemical residues, fungal mycelia, and insect droppings.
- **Moisture Retention Capacity (MRC)**: The physiological ability of a leaf to hold moisture over 6–12 hours after harvest. High MRC prevents drying out in the rearing bed.

## 4. Engineering Relevance
- **Edge Cut Quality & Bruising**: Dull blades crush cell walls, exuding cellular sap. This sap oxidizes quickly (browning), promotes bacterial growth, and causes moisture loss along the cut line up to 3× faster than clean shear cuts.
- **Cutting Energy vs. Moisture**: High-moisture leaves require sharp shearing blades; dull blades tear pliable tissue instead of severing it, causing machine clogging and frayed feed edges.
- **Optical Inspection Parameters**: Vision systems can inspect color (chlorophyll degradation, chlorosis), reflectance (wilted vs. turgid leaves), and surface blemishes to detect unwholesome feed.

## 5. Questions to Investigate
- What is the quantitative rate of moisture loss (wt%/hr) at 25°C and 70% RH for cleanly sheared leaves versus torn/bruised leaves?
- Can spectral reflectance or RGB color space analysis reliably differentiate fresh high-moisture leaves from wilted or senescent leaves?
- What maximum allowable mechanical pressure can feed rollers exert on leaves without causing cell rupture and sap expression?
