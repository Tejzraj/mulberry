# 01 - Mulberry Domain Knowledge

This directory documents the biological, agronomic, and physical characteristics of the mulberry (*Morus* spp.) plant relevant to feed preparation in sericulture.

## Document Index

1. [`mulberry-plant.md`](mulberry-plant.md) — Botanical classification, anatomy, leaf morphology, and physical structure.
2. [`mulberry-cultivation.md`](mulberry-cultivation.md) — Agronomic practices, pruning cycles, spacing, and irrigation schedules.
3. [`mulberry-varieties.md`](mulberry-varieties.md) — Prevalent sericultural cultivars (e.g., V-1, S-36, G-4) and morphological differences.
4. [`mulberry-leaf-quality.md`](mulberry-leaf-quality.md) — Moisture, protein, carbohydrate content, succulent leaf indices, and feeding suitability.
5. [`mulberry-harvesting.md`](mulberry-harvesting.md) — Leaf plucking vs. shoot harvesting methods, preservation, and post-harvest handling.
6. [`mulberry-pests-and-diseases.md`](mulberry-pests-and-diseases.md) — Common pathogens (powdery mildew, leaf spot) and pests (thrips, mealybugs) affecting feed quality.
7. [`glossary.md`](glossary.md) — Key botanical, agricultural, and sericultural terminology.

## Research Objectives

- Characterize the mechanical and physical properties of mulberry stems and leaves (moisture %, stem diameter, tensile/shear resistance).
- Quantify how variety, maturity, and harvest method affect cutting resistance and blade wear.
- Identify visual and physical indicators of leaf degradation or disease for machine-vision inspection.
