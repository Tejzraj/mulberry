# Mulberry Plant (*Morus* spp.)

## 1. What It Is
Mulberry is a fast-growing deciduous perennial woody tree or shrub belonging to the genus *Morus* of the family Moraceae. In sericulture, it serves as the sole primary host plant and nutritional source for the domesticated silkworm, *Bombyx mori* L.

## 2. Why It Matters
Silkworms are monophagous insects whose growth, silk gland development, and subsequent cocoon quality depend exclusively on the nutritional biochemical components and moisture of the ingested mulberry foliage.

## 3. Key Concepts
- **Taxonomy**: Kingdom Plantae, Order Rosales, Family Moraceae, Genus *Morus*. Major species cultivated in India include *Morus alba*, *Morus indica*, *Morus latifolia*, and *Morus nigra*.
- **Plant Habit**: Maintained as low bushes (0.5 to 1.5 m) or small trees through systematic bottom pruning to promote high succulent foliage yield.
- **Leaf Structure**: Simple, alternate, ovate to cordate, petiolate, often lobed or unlobed depending on variety and node position. Leaves have a distinct petiole, midrib, secondary veins, and succulent mesophyll tissue.
- **Shoot/Stem Anatomy**: Composed of outer epidermal bark, green cortical tissue, phloem, vascular cambium, xylem (fibrous wood), and a soft central pith. Lignification increases markedly from the apical shoot down toward the base.

## 4. Engineering Relevance
- **Dual Material Characteristics**: The cutting system must process two distinct mechanical regimes: pliable, moist, high-surface-area leaf laminae vs. fibrous, woody, anisotropic stems/shoots.
- **Shear Resistance Variations**: Green apical stems have high moisture and low cutting resistance, whereas basal shoots have dense lignified xylem requiring substantially higher cutting torque.
- **Petiole and Vein Toughness**: The blade must cleanly sever tough fibrovascular bundles in petioles and midribs without chewing, folding, or tearing the surrounding laminae.

## 5. Questions to Investigate
- What is the shear strength (in MPa or N/mm²) of fresh mulberry stems across diameters from 2 mm to 15 mm?
- How does leaf blade thickness and petiole diameter vary across standard sericultural cultivars (e.g., V-1 vs. S-36)?
- How rapidly does stem cut resistance increase as the shoot ages between bottom pruning cycles?
