# Mulberry Harvesting

## 1. What It Is
Mulberry harvesting is the process of collecting feed material from the garden for silkworm rearing, carried out through individual leaf picking or whole shoot cutting.

## 2. Why It Matters
The harvest method and timing determine the physical format, stem content, temperature, and moisture level of the input material entering the processing or cutting operation.

## 3. Key Concepts
- **Harvest Methods**:
  1. *Individual Leaf Picking*: Leaves are plucked individually by hand from the plant nodes. Practiced primarily for 1st and 2nd instar chawki rearing where tender, top-tier leaves are selected.
  2. *Branch / Shoot Harvesting*: Whole shoots are cut 10–15 cm above ground level using pruning shears, secateurs, or sickles. Practiced primarily for 4th and 5th instars (shoot rearing) to reduce labor.
- **Harvest Timing**: Carried out during the cool morning hours (before 8:00 AM) or late afternoon to minimize transpirational wilting and leaf moisture depletion under direct sunlight.
- **Post-Harvest Leaf Preservation**: Harvested material is transported in wet gunny bags or placed in dark, humid leaf storage chambers (covered with wet cloths) to sustain turgor and minimize leaf temperature.

## 4. Engineering Relevance
- **Input Feed Topography**:
  - Plucked leaf feed consists of unattached, loose, flexible sheets with petioles.
  - Shoot feed consists of bundles of rigid branches (3–15 mm diameter) with attached lateral leaves at varying angles.
- **Feeder Mechanism Demands**: A universal machine must handle both loose leaf feeding (requiring conveyor belts or hopper feeds) and branch feeding (requiring powered nip rollers to draw in rigid shoots).
- **Feed Pre-sorting**: Branch harvesting carries dirt, field grit, and damaged lower leaves that can dull blades prematurely or introduce pathogens into rearing trays.

## 5. Questions to Investigate
- How does the feed rate of loose leaves through mechanical hoppers compare to the feed rate of guided shoots?
- What is the effect of post-harvest storage time (1 to 6 hours) on leaf shear resistance and blade gumming from sap?
- Are shoot harvest practices standardizing across modern sericulture farms in target regions?
