# Mulberry Domain Glossary

A reference glossary of botanical, agronomic, and physical terms used throughout the project.

| Term | Definition | Context in Project |
|---|---|---|
| **Moriculture** | The scientific cultivation and management of mulberry plants for sericulture. | Field operations supplying the input biomass. |
| **Lamina** | The expanded, flat portion of the mulberry leaf blade responsible for photosynthesis. | Primary edible biomass for silkworms; delicate and prone to moisture loss. |
| **Petiole** | The stalk attaching the leaf blade to the branch or stem. | Tough fibrovascular structure requiring clean shear cutting. |
| **Lignin / Lignification** | Complex organic polymer deposited in cell walls of xylem, imparting rigidity and woodiness. | Increases shear force required for cutting; primary source of blade wear. |
| **Shoot Rearing** | Feeding late-age silkworms with cut branch shoots rather than hand-picked leaves. | Dictates mechanical requirement to process bundled woody stems and leaves together. |
| **Chawki** | Silkworms in their first two larval instars (1st and 2nd instars), requiring tender feed. | Demands finely chopped, high-moisture, bruise-free leaf squares. |
| **Bottom Pruning** | Cutting mulberry branches 10–15 cm above the ground at the end of each cropping cycle. | Produces standard uniform shoot flushes harvested every 50–60 days. |
| **Tukra** | A malformation disease in mulberry caused by the mealybug (*Maconellicoccus hirsutus*). | Target visual defect for automated computer vision detection and rejection. |
| **Turgor Pressure** | Hydrostatic pressure exerted by cellular fluids against the plant cell wall. | High turgor enables crisp cutting; low turgor leads to tearing and folding. |
| **Succulent Leaf** | Tender leaf with high moisture (> 75%) and protein, low fiber, suitable for young larvae. | Material benchmark for Chawki feed preparation. |
| **Moisture Retention Capacity (MRC)** | Metric measuring percentage of water retained in leaf tissue over time post-harvest. | Quality metric evaluated to assess cut edge damage and feed shelf-life. |
| **Specific Cutting Energy** | Energy required to cut a unit cross-sectional area or mass of plant material ($J/mm^2$ or $J/kg$). | Core engineering metric for motor and drivetrain sizing. |
