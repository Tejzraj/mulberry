## 3. Candidate Search Spaces
Prior-art searches will be conducted systematically across the following jurisdictions:
- **Indian Patent Advanced Search System (InPASS)**: Focus on Indian patent applications under Class `A01F`, `A01D`, `A01K`.
- **WIPO Patentscope**: Global Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT) applications.
- **Google Patents & Espacenet (EPO)**: US, European, Japanese, and Chinese agricultural machine patents.
- **China National Intellectual Property Administration (CNIPA)**: China is the world's largest sericulture producer; a significant volume of sericulture mechanization patents exists in Chinese patent literature.
