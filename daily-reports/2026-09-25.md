## Key Understanding
- **Biological Constraint on Mechanics**: Silkworm mouthpart mechanics shift radically across 5 instars (from delicate biting of 0.5 cm leaf squares in 1st instar Chawki to consuming entire 12 mm woody shoots in 5th instar). The cutting machine must either offer continuously adjustable cut length or discrete biological presets without requiring manual mechanical reconfiguration.
- **Cellular Bruising Mechanism**: Dull blades or high-clearance shear bars crush leaf mesophyll cells rather than severing them, causing cellular fluid exudation, rapid oxidation (black borders), and accelerated moisture loss that suppresses larval ingestion.
- **Closed-Loop Control Necessity**: Open-loop motorized choppers inevitably stall, jam, or damage blades when processing heterogeneous mulberry biomass. Real-time motor current sensing and independent variable-speed feed rollers provide a pathway to automated jam prevention and density adaptation.
- **Patentability Realities**: Standalone software and algorithmic models are barred under Section 3(k) of the Indian Patents Act; any viable IP must be anchored in the physical interaction between electromechanical actuators and biological comminution. Patentability requires formal prior-art and legal assessment.
