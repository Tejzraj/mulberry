# Daily Report — 2026-09-27

## Objective
Adopt a modular **Mini Model First** engineering strategy for the project: *“Design and Development of a Compact Electric Plant Cutting, Gathering and Binding Machine for Future Mulberry Application”*.

---

## Strategic Shift & Rationale
- **Mini Model Proof of Concept**: Rather than attempting immediate high-torque cutting of mature, woody mulberry branches (which poses severe mechanical risk, high motor stall hazards, and large fabrication expenses), the team decided to design, fabricate, and test a low-cost, compact electric **Prototype-1** platform.
- **Test Vegetation**: Small and soft-stem plants such as coriander, Congress grass (*Parthenium hysterophorus*), and tender garden vegetation.
- **Operational Sequence Validated**:
  `PLANT ──> GUIDE ──> CUT ──> GATHER ──> ALIGN ──> COMPRESS ──> BIND ──> RELEASE BUNDLE`
- **Future Mulberry Adaptation**: Once the cutting, conveying, alignment, and binding mechanisms are physically proven, the modular cutter and transmission will be upgraded for woody mulberry stems without redesigning the chassis or power bus.
