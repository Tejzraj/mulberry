# Project Status & Current Milestones

> **Project Title**: Design and Development of a Compact Electric Plant Cutting, Gathering and Binding Machine for Future Mulberry Application  
> **Status**: Concept / Prototype-1 Engineering Phase  
> **Current Strategy**: Mini Model First (Stage 1 Proof of Concept)  
> **Last Updated**: 2026-09-27  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The project is structured around a **mini-model first development philosophy**:
```
PLANT → GUIDE → CUT → GATHER → ALIGN → COMPRESS → BIND → RELEASE BUNDLE
```
Rather than attempting immediate high-power cutting of mature mulberry shoots, **Prototype-1** is engineered for soft-stem vegetation (coriander, Congress grass) to validate the complex electromechanical coordination of cutting, gathering, compression, and automated binding at low mechanical risk and within a **₹45,000 budget**.
